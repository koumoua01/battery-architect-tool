Tools

Paint: drag over cells to join them to the highlighted island. Shift paints the whole row, right-click erases.

Current

Pick − or +, then click a pad. The end belongs to that sheet's island; both ends may sit on either sheet.

Rows

    − and + take one cell off or add one to that row. Hold Shift to work on the left end instead of the right.

    Face

    The bottom sheet is the exact mirror of the top; positions are edited on the top view only. The box draws the other plate dashed, through this one, where it lies in the pack.

    Islands — colour = one piece

    Click a coloured group to select it, then Remove group (− beside the +, or I) deletes it. Its cells stay in the pack.

    Geometry

    Plate: one solid piece per island, the union of every pad and strap — strongest, and the shortest current path.
